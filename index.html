Tutorial Gmail, Odoo and your Google profile picture

Add it in Gmail

  1. Click Copy for Gmail at the top of this card.
  2. Open Gmail, click the gear icon at the top right and choose See all settings.
  3. In the General tab, scroll down to Signature.
  4. Click + Create new, name it (e.g. TechNext), then click Create.
  5. Click inside the signature box and paste with Ctrl + V (Cmd + V on Mac).
  6. Under Signature defaults, pick your new signature for For new emails and On reply/forward.
  7. Scroll to the very bottom and click Save Changes.
The logo and icons load from the web automatically. Just paste; don’t re-upload any images.

Add it in Odoo

  1. Click Copy for Odoo at the top of this card.
  2. In Odoo, add ?debug=1 to the end of the address and press Enter. This turns on developer mode.
  3. Click your avatar in the top right and open your preferences.
  4. Click inside the Signature box, then double-click any word so the formatting toolbar appears.
  5. Click the </> icon at the right-hand end of that toolbar. The box switches to plain HTML.
  6. Press Ctrl + A, then Delete, then paste with Ctrl + V.
  7. Click Save, then send a message from any CRM lead to an outside address to check it.
Always paste into the </> code view. Pasting into the normal box strips the formatting and blows the logo up to full size.

Change your profile picture

This changes your Google / Gmail account photo only: the picture people see when you email from your @technext.asia account. Other apps are not affected.
  1. Open Gmail and click your profile photo in the top-right circle.
  2. Click the camera icon on your photo, or Manage your Google Account.
  3. Go to Personal info, then click your photo under “Profile picture”.
  4. Click Add profile picture (or Change) and upload your image.
  5. Crop or reposition it, then click Save as profile picture.
Animated GIFs play in Google Chat, Meet and opened emails; the inbox list shows their first frame. It can take a few minutes to update everywhere.

Tutorial Make your photo and set it on Gmail

Make the photo

  1. Remove the background from a sharp head-and-shoulders photo (for example with remove.bg or Canva) and save it as a PNG.
  2. Click Upload your photo and choose the PNG. It is placed in the ring straight away.
  3. Drag the photo so your head sits under the logo, and zoom until your shoulders fill the bottom of the ring.
  4. Pick the ring and background, then click Download HD.

Use it

  1. Set it as your Google / Gmail profile picture (steps in the email signature tutorial).
  2. Use the same file on LinkedIn, Odoo, Teams and WhatsApp so you look the same everywhere.
Gmail crops photos to a circle; the ring sits right on that circle, so it frames your photo there too. Natural daylight from a window in front of you gives the cleanest result.

Tutorial Gmail profile, slides and email

As your Gmail profile picture

  1. Download the Gmail profile size GIF (light or blue) above.
  2. In Gmail, click your profile photo in the top-right circle, then Manage your Google Account.
  3. Go to Personal info and click your photo under “Profile picture”.
  4. Click Change, upload the GIF, keep the plane centred in the circle and click Save as profile picture.
The animation plays in Google Chat, Meet, your expanded profile and opened emails. The inbox list shows the first frame, which is the finished logo.

In a presentation (PowerPoint / Google Slides)

  1. Click Download light or Download blue to save the 512 px GIF.
  2. In your slide, go to Insert → Image (or Insert → Picture) and pick the GIF.
  3. Resize and position it. It animates automatically in Slide Show / Present mode.

In an email

  1. In Gmail, click Insert photo in the compose toolbar and upload the GIF (choose Inline).
  2. Send yourself a test email first to check the animation plays.
Most email apps and slide software play the animation. A few older email apps show only the first frame; that’s normal.

Tutorial Zoom, Teams and Google Meet

Zoom

  1. Download a background above.
  2. Open Settings → Background & Effects.
  3. Under Virtual Backgrounds, click +, then Add Image, and pick the file.

Microsoft Teams

  1. Before or during a call, click More (…) → Video effects and settings / Background effects.
  2. Click Add new, upload the background, select it and click Apply.

Google Meet

  1. In the preview or during a call, click Apply visual effects (the sparkle icon).
  2. Under Backgrounds, click the + tile and upload the background.
Not sure which to pick? Start with Normal. If the logo looks mirrored in your own preview, that’s just your self-view; others see it the right way round.
